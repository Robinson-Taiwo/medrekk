---
title: "MedRekk"
description: "An offline-first, patient-owned medical record."
---

A patient's medical history normally belongs to whichever hospital wrote it down. Walk into a
different clinic and it starts again — and when the network is down, none of it is reachable at
all.

MedRekk puts the record on the patient's own phone. It works with no connection, a health worker
sees only what the patient approves and only for as long as they allow, and every look is written
into a log the patient alone can read.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Nothing shared by default" icon="lock">
    A clinician holding a patient's code can request access and nothing more. The patient sees who
    is asking, where they work and why — and may approve fewer areas than were requested.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Access ends by itself" icon="clock">
    Every grant carries an expiry. Nobody has to remember to revoke it, which is the step that
    never happens in practice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every read recorded" icon="eye">
    Views, emergency scans and USSD dial-ins all land in a trail only the patient can open.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works without a smartphone" icon="phone">
    Any record can be read by dialling a USSD code and entering a PIN. The emergency card is
    readable from a QR with no approval at all.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where things live

| Repository | What it is | Deployed to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`medrekk`](https://github.com/Robinson-Taiwo/medrekk) | The mobile app, for patients and health workers | EAS Build (Android APK) |
| [`medrekk-backend`](https://github.com/Robinson-Taiwo/medrekk-backend) | API, consent engine, audit trail | Rumpty Cloud |
| [`medrekk-web`](https://github.com/Robinson-Taiwo/medrekk-web) | Site, clinician reader, emergency lookup, USSD simulator, admin | Rumpty Cloud |

<Info>
They are deliberately separate rather than a monorepo. A 25-minute native build and a Next.js
deploy share no pipeline, and coupling them would mean every web change invalidating a mobile
build cache.
</Info>
